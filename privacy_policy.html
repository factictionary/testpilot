2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on the app, and interactions with content.
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise GPS location).

  • Log Data: Server logs containing IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Sign-In: If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account information (name, email, profile picture).
  • Payment Providers: Transaction information from payment processors.
  • Analytics Services: Usage data from Firebase Analytics and similar services.
