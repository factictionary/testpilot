Frequently Asked Questions

What is Test Pilot Pro? +
Test Pilot Pro is a platform that connects Android developers with verified testers to fulfill Google Play's closed testing requirements. We provide 12 testers for 14 days at just ₹299.
How long does testing take? +
Testers are typically assigned within 24 hours. The testing period is 14 days for the Premium plan and 7 days for the Free Trial.
Is my app data secure? +
Yes! We use enterprise-grade encryption, secure servers, and follow GDPR compliance. Your app data is never shared with third parties.
Can I get a refund? +
All payments are non-refundable once testing has commenced. However, if you have issues, contact our support team within 7 days of payment.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI payments and manual payment verification via screenshot. Both methods are secure and processed quickly.
Can I see tester identities? +
No, tester identities are anonymous for privacy. Testers are identified as Tester1, Tester2, etc. You'll see their feedback and ratings.
