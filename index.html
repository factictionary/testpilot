Why DeviceLab12

Google's rule is unforgiving. We make it cheap.

Personal developer accounts need opted-in testers who keep your app installed and engaged for 14 continuous days before you can apply for Production. If even one tester drops out, the clock resets.

  • No bot farms. Every device shows authentic hardware identifiers and daily session logs.
  • No "Item not found" 404s. Pre-configured Google Group eliminates tester opt-in friction.
  • No manual chasing. Reciprocal dev-swap keeps testers motivated by their own roadmap.
  • No surprises at review. Export comprehensive logs to answer every production-application question.
How it works

Four steps from Play Console to Production-ready

  1. 1

    Set up your closed track

    In Google Play Console, add testpilotpro-testers@googlegroups.com under Manage Testers.

  2. 2

    Submit your app

    Enter your app name, package name, and Play Store testing-track link inside DeviceLab12.

  3. 3

    Choose a plan

    Allocate your earned Dev Credits or activate the VIP Lab Fleet for all 12 dedicated devices.

  4. 4

    Monitor & ship

    Watch daily device check-ins, read tester feedback, and hit your 14-day milestone ready for Production.
